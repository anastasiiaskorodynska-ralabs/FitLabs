FitLabs

Design system

Focused, strong, calm. Built for one thumb, between sets, at arm's length. Dark first, light ready, English and Ukrainian.

Arm's-length legible
Numbers you act on are 40px+. Nothing essential below 15px.
One thumb
Targets 44px minimum, 56px default. Primary actions live in the bottom third.
One accent
Pink is only for the next action. Type colours label; they never ask you to tap.
Room for Ukrainian
Plan for +35% text length. Labels wrap to 2 lines; no fixed-width text boxes.
